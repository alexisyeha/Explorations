# Day 002 — Mobile

A portrait iPhone prototype showing only the living wall-shadow of an off-camera mobile. Six softly projected abstract marks can be caught, dragged, and released. The two stacked left marks form one articulated thread and the three stacked right marks form another. Every shape pivots separately from its pierced attachment while passing a softer impulse to its neighbors, so the continuous thread flexes without behaving like a rigid column. Their movement transfers into the two unseen hand-bent bars, while each touched mark plays its own locally synthesized chime.

The visible shapes range from 18 to 56 points. Their interactive hit areas remain at least 56 points—even when the structure fits itself onto a shorter iPhone—so the composition can stay delicate without becoming difficult to touch. A touch gives the bars a small physical nudge; dragging flexes the illustrated wire and transfers a larger impulse through the structure.

A warm ivory wall shifts gently from bright left to pale right. Sparse sun-caustic pools and tiny prismatic flecks drift behind the mobile on a slow independent current. The source mobile never enters the frame: a softly blurred warm-charcoal projection follows one continuous wind cycle, swinging through a roughly 90° arc and back while its connected lower assembly trails with a subtle delay.
